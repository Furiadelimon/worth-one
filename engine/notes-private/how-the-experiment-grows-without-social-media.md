title: How the experiment grows without social media
date: 2026-09-12
summary: The distribution plan, published in advance: search, sharing, referrals, embeds, directories, newsletters, publishers. What is automated, what is not, and what gets killed.
---
Worth One? has no social media accounts and does not plan to open any for now. That is a constraint chosen on purpose: if the drops cannot travel on their own merits, through people and search, they are not worth a euro. This note is the plan, so that results can be checked against it later.

## The loop the whole thing depends on

Visitor → uses a drop → gets a result → shares it → new visitor. Every drop ships with the pieces that make that loop possible: an image of the result built on your device, a compare-with-a-friend link, and native share plus WhatsApp, Telegram, email and copy-link. The share link carries an anonymous referral id so the experiment can measure how many new people each user brings (the K factor). K above 1 means a drop grows on its own; that is the only kind of growth worth scaling.

## Search

Each drop answers a question people already type: how much time do I spend scrolling, how much do subscriptions cost over ten years. The drop pages are written for those questions, and a small set of reference pages (screen time by the hour, subscriptions one by one) answers the long-tail versions with real content rather than filler. New pages are only added when a query shows demand. Sitemaps and IndexNow submissions are automated; the rest is patience.

## Embeds

Any site can [embed a drop](../embed/) with one line of HTML, free, with a discreet "Powered by Worth One?" line. Widget views are counted anonymously by hostname so it is clear where the tools are useful.

## Directories, newsletters, publishers

Only relevant ones, only with a personalised note that explains why the drop fits their readers, one message per contact ever, never bulk. Most "free tool" directories turned out to be paid or dead when checked, so the list is short and honest. Every contact and its outcome is recorded.

## What is automated

Analytics, reports, sitemaps, indexing pings, the daily metrics loop, and a reasoning step that proposes ideas, writes drafts and decides what to scale or kill based on numbers. What is not automated: posting to communities, sending email without a project mailbox, and anything that requires pretending to be someone.

## What gets killed

A drop with enough qualified traffic and a low share rate, low "worth €1" rate and low repeat use is demoted to a cheap static asset and stops receiving effort. A channel that costs time and brings no users is paused. Aggregate results (never individual ones) will be published here as milestones are reached: first 1,000 users, first 25 countries, first drop with K above 1.
