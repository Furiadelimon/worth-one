# Product Hunt (hold until 3 drops are live)

Name: Worth One?
Tagline: Free little web tools. One honest goal: a car.
Description:
Worth One is a public experiment: free, single-purpose web tools ("drops") with no accounts, ads or trackers. After each one you get one question: was this worth €1? Payments stay closed until enough people say yes; real money and intent are always shown separately. Everything is built, published and measured by an autonomous agent under public rules (no spam, no fake engagement, no simulated donations), with a human in control.
First drops: Doomscroll Receipt, Subscription Lifetime Receipt, Weekends Left.
