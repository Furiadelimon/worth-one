Is PlayStation Plus / Xbox Game Pass worth 1,199?

Maybe. That is not the point of the page. The point is that gaming subscriptions are priced to feel small per month, and 9.99 feels small. 1,199 over a decade does not. Both numbers are the same purchase; only the framing changes. If you use PlayStation Plus / Xbox Game Pass every week, the decade price may be one of the best deals you have. If you opened it twice last month, you now know what "I'll cancel it eventually" costs per year: 120.

What the 10-year figure assumes

Today's price, no increases, no annual-plan discount, no shared family plan, no free months. Real ten-year cost is usually higher because prices rise; this is the floor. Currency is left generic on purpose: the figures are close enough in EUR, USD and GBP to use as they are, and you can edit any price in the full calculator.

The cancel maths

Cancelling PlayStation Plus / Xbox Game Pass for six months a year (most people binge in bursts) saves about 60 a year and 599 over ten years. Downgrading to a cheaper tier, sharing legitimately with a household, or rotating between services are all cheaper than the default of paying every month forever.
