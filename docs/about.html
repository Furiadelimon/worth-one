About

No company. One person.

I make small free things for the internet. Some useful, some strange, some just fun. This page is the long version, for anyone who wants it.

What this is

A small collection of tools you can use in ten seconds without signing up for anything: a receipt for your scrolling, a receipt for your subscriptions, daily word games from the same kitchen, and whatever gets made next. They are free and they stay free. No ads, no account, nothing to install, nothing you type is uploaded.

Why a car?

There are no investors and no master plan. At some point I wondered whether enough small useful things could eventually pay for something very real, and a used car was the most honest thing I could think of. So that is the experiment: make things, give them away, and after each one ask a single question. Was this worth €1 to you?

Right now that question only records an answer. No payments are open and nothing is charged. If enough people ever say yes, a normal payment provider will be connected and voluntary contributions will open, and the real total will be shown on the home page next to the answers. Target: €30,000. Real money received so far: €0.

What it is not: not an investment, not a company, not charity, not crowdfunding for a product that comes later. The tools exist first. A contribution, if that day comes, is a small gift for something that already gave you something.

Who is behind it?

One person, deliberately not named. The tools should be judged on whether they are worth a minute of your time, not on who made them. Software does a lot of the routine work, under simple rules: no spam, no fake accounts, no bought attention, no fake numbers.

Why would I share one?

Because the results are personal and slightly ridiculous, which is exactly what people send each other. Every tool builds a share image on your device and gives you a link that lets a friend compare with you. Sharing is the only thing this project asks for.

You can also put any tool on your own site with one line of HTML, free.

FAQ

Is anything charged when I click "Worth €1"?

No. Nothing. The button records an answer only. Payments are closed until a human decides to open them, and the home page will say so clearly if that happens.

Why a car and not something noble?

Because it is true. A fake noble cause would be worse than an honest small one. You are never asked to care about the car; you are asked whether a free tool was worth €1.

Who are you?

One person, not named. To reach the project, open an issue on GitHub.

What happens to my data?

Very little. An anonymous random id in your browser, the page you viewed, the country your connection comes from, and your answers. No names, no ads, no third-party trackers. If you leave an email, it is used for exactly what it says next to the box and nothing else. Details on the privacy page.

What if the target is never reached?

Then the tools were not worth it, and that is a valid result. Everything stays free anyway.

Can I suggest a tool, or embed one?

Yes and yes. Suggest via GitHub issues; embed via the embed page.

Terms, briefly

The tools are provided as they are, for entertainment and general information, without warranties. Numbers are arithmetic on what you type, not professional advice of any kind. Contributions, if ever enabled, are voluntary gifts to a private person with no goods, services, rights or returns attached, and are not refundable except where the law requires it.
