Press kit

Worth One? in one paragraph.

Worth One? is a small collection of free internet tools made by one person: a receipt for your scrolling, a receipt for your subscriptions, daily word games, and more as they are made. No account, no ads, nothing uploaded. After each tool you can say whether it was worth €1; nothing is charged. Behind it is an honest, slightly odd experiment: whether enough small useful things could eventually pay for one used car.

Facts

  • What: free, single-purpose browser tools. Ten to twenty seconds each.
  • Who: one person, not named on purpose. No company, no investors.
  • Price: free. No account. No ads. No payments are open.
  • Languages: English and Spanish.
  • Embeddable: yes, one line of HTML, free. Details.
  • Source: github.com/Furiadelimon/worth-one (MIT).
  • Contact: hello@wordsbeforecoffee.com

One-liners you may use
