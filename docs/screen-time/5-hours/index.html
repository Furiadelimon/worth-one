5 hours a day is 76 days a year.

What 5 hours a day means in practice

5 hours of scrolling a day is 1,826 hours a year. Expressed in full 24-hour days it is 76 days, which is easier to feel: it is the length of a long holiday, spent in roughly 300-minute pieces you will not remember individually.

Over ten years the same habit is 18,262 hours. That is enough time to read about 3,044 books at six hours each, to learn 36 languages to a conversational level (roughly 500 hours each), or to watch every episode of Friends 209.9 times.

Compared with everyone else

The world average for social media is about 2 hours 20 minutes a day (DataReportal, Digital 2025). 5 hours a day is 115% above that average, which puts you in the "Heavy rotation" tier on the receipt. The tier names are a joke; the numbers are not.

The refund department

Cutting just 30 minutes a day from 5 hours gives back 7.6 days a year and 76 days over ten years, without changing anything else. Cutting it to one hour a day would return 61 days a year. None of this is advice; it is arithmetic you can act on or ignore.

How to check your real number

iPhone: Settings → Screen Time → See All App & Website Activity, weekly view, add up social and entertainment apps and divide by seven. Android: Settings → Digital Wellbeing → Dashboard. Then print your own receipt and send it to the friend who claims they "barely use their phone".
