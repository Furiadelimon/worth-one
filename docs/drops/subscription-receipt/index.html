What this subscription cost calculator does

You tap the subscriptions you pay for, correct the prices, and it prints the monthly and yearly total, then the 5, 10 and 20-year cost. The 10-year line is converted into things people can picture: a share of a car, coffees, weeks of groceries, flights. There is no inflation adjustment and no investment fantasy; it is only what leaves your account at today's prices.

Why the 10-year line matters

Subscriptions are priced to feel small per month. Nobody signs up for "€1,680 of streaming per decade", but that is what €14 a month is. Seeing the decade total next to the monthly price is usually enough to cancel one thing, which is the entire purpose of this page.

How to use it honestly

Include the ones you forget: cloud storage, an app you no longer open, the second streaming service, the phone insurance. Then remove one and watch the receipt reprint. Repeat until the number stops bothering you.
