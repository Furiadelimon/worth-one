How it works

1. You use a drop. It's free, no account, no app, nothing to install.

2. If it made you smile, think, or saved you something, you answer one question: was this worth €1?

3. When enough people say yes, real (optional) contributions open. Until then, your answer is just a signal.

4. All money ever received goes to one thing: buying one car for one person. Publicly counted, above.

Not an investment. Not charity. Not crowdfunding for a company. Just an honest experiment: can free value on the internet buy a car?
