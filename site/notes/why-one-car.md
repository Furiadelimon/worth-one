title: Why one car, and why we say so
date: 2026-09-12
summary: The premise of the experiment, stated plainly: free things for strangers, one person, one car, no hidden price, no identity, no promises.
---
Most free things on the internet are not free. The price is attention, data, ads, or a subscription that starts the moment you forget to cancel. This experiment tries the opposite arrangement and says its purpose out loud.

## The arrangement

One person publishes small web tools ("drops") that anyone can use instantly, without an account or an app. After using one, you get a single question: was this worth €1 to you? You can answer yes at one, three or five euros, or no. Nothing is charged. Right now the answers are only a signal.

If enough people say yes, a normal payment provider will be connected and voluntary contributions will open. Every euro received goes to one thing: buying one car, target €30,000. Real money will always be shown separately from intended money, on the homepage, publicly.

## Why a car

Because it is true, and because a true small goal is better than a fake noble one. Nobody is asked to care about the car. The only question ever asked is whether a free thing was worth a euro. The car is just the honest answer to "where does the money go".

## Why the person is not named

The tools should be judged on whether they are worth it, not on who made them. So the public identity is the project itself, WORTH ONE?, and the person behind it stays unnamed.

## What it is not

- Not an investment, not equity, nothing to own.
- Not a company, not an organisation, not a charity.
- Not crowdfunding for a product that will exist later. The tools exist first, free, forever.
- Not an emergency. Nobody needs the car. It is a goal, not a plea.

## How it grows

By being worth sending to someone. No ads, no bought attention. If a tool is useful it travels; if not, it is removed.

## The bet

That genuinely free value, spread by people who liked it, can add up to something real. If it cannot, that is a result too, and everything stays free anyway.
